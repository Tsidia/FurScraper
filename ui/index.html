FurAffinity access

        

          

            Your FA account must use the Classic theme.
            The Modern layout cannot be read at all. On FA go to Settings → Site
            Preferences and choose Classic.
          

          

            Log into furaffinity.net, press F12, open Application → Cookies →
            www.furaffinity.net, and copy the values of the cookies named
            a and b. Every FA source needs these; your
            session is what authenticates each request.
